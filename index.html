Privacy Policy

Skip Sensei · Last updated October 5, 2026

Skip Sensei is a jump rope workout app. It counts your jumps with a supported smart jump rope or your phone's camera, coaches you with animated characters, and saves your workout history. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: your workouts and camera images stay on your phone, there are no ads, no analytics, and no account.

Information the app uses

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Camera images Only when you turn on camera tracking: to count your jumps and give form tips. Processed on your phone in real time. Images are never saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.
Bluetooth connection to a smart jump rope To read your jump count and the rope's battery level. Stays on your phone. The app does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
Workout history (dates, jumps, duration, rounds) To show your past sessions. Stored only on your phone.
Body weight (optional) Only if you enter it on the Progress screen: to estimate a calorie range for your workouts. Stored only on your phone.
Welcome questions (goal, experience level, whether you have a rope, reminder preference, jumps tried) and your chosen coach To personalize your plan and greet you. Stored only on your phone.
Subscription purchases To unlock Pro features you pay for. Handled by Google Play and our subscription service, RevenueCat (see below).

Health Connect

If you turn on "Sync to Health Connect" in the app, Skip Sensei writes each finished workout to Health Connect on your phone: one exercise session (start and end time, workout name, and its rounds as segments) and an estimate of active calories burned. The app only writes this data; it never reads anything from Health Connect. Nothing is written unless you turn the option on and grant permission, and you can turn it off or revoke the permissions at any time in the app or in Health Connect's settings. Data in Health Connect is stored on your device and managed by Android under Google's privacy policy; other apps can only see it if you give them permission there.

Skip Sensei's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts from Google Play, and basic device information such as app version and platform. RevenueCat does not receive your name, workouts, or camera data. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy and Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

Manage or cancel your subscription any time in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No account or sign-in.
  • No microphone access. Coach voices are prerecorded audio.
  • No location access.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Deleting your data

Because your workouts, answers, and settings live on your phone, you can delete all of them by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. Workouts already written to Health Connect can be deleted in Health Connect's settings. To ask RevenueCat to delete your anonymous purchase record, contact us at the address below.

Children

Skip Sensei is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, mention it in the app.
